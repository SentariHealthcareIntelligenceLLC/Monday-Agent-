    Reminder rules

    Set once, applied to every published shift at every facility.

    1. Clock in. 10 minutes before shift start. If nothing is punched 5 minutes after start, the person is texted again and marked running late to the administration.
    2. Lunch out. 4 hours into the shift, if no break has been taken.
    3. Lunch in. 30 minutes after lunch out — a missed return is a wage-and-hour problem, so this one escalates.
    4. Clock out. At shift end, then every 15 minutes until punched, so nobody accidentally banks overtime.
    5. Digest. End of day, the administration gets attendance, late punches and missed breaks in one message.

    How the reminder agent behaves

    Straight from the Aug 18 planning call.

    1. Two pushes a day. 8:00 AM and 2:00 PM local, per assignee, over WhatsApp.
    2. It doesn’t stop. If the person doesn’t reply done, the same reminder repeats the next morning and afternoon, and the next, until they do.
    3. It tells them how. Each reminder carries the short “what to do / how to do it” note attached to the task, so nobody has to ask.
    4. “Done” is a timestamp. The reply writes name + date + time to the board — that log is what you hand a surveyor asking for proof the step was performed.
    5. Escalation. 4 unanswered reminders → the Manager is notified. 6 → the Owner is notified. Both land in “Behind on tasks”.
    6. You pick how often. Every duty and every person carries a reminder frequency — twice a day, once a day, every other day, once a week, every other week, or once a month. Set a default on someone’s profile; override it on any individual duty with the ⏱ button. Nothing is one-size-fits-all.
    7. One dashboard, many facilities. The facility switcher at the top runs one site on its own, or several together as a combined view. Manage facilities to rename sites, add rooms and ORs, or build your own group — a single owner running three facilities sees them as one board or one at a time.
    8. Three ways to close a duty. A planner duty is closed when the person replies DONE, ticks the box on the dashboard, or uploads the photo. Duties marked 📷 — crash cart, autoclave log, deep clean, facility checklist, drills — are not closed by a tick alone; the picture has to be uploaded on the day of the duty. Until one of those happens, the daily texts keep going to the person and to Fredshon, and to Karina once it carries past its day.
    9. Planners are work duty, not coverage. The weekly and monthly planners assign jobs to people on specific days. Who is staffing which room is a separate thing and lives in the Scheduling tab.
    10. Recurring duties chase themselves. Every profile carries standing responsibilities in five boxes — weekly, monthly, quarterly, every 6 months, yearly. Each opens its own reminder window ahead of the due date (2 days for weekly, 5 monthly, 14 quarterly, 30 semi-annual, 45 yearly). Once open, the agent texts the person and Fredshon every day at 8:00 AM. Past due, Karina is copied too. Marking it done timestamps it, stops the reminders, and schedules the next occurrence automatically.
    11. Credentials chase themselves. Every expiry date is watched. A credential turns Expiring at 60 days for visibility; at 30 days out the daily reminder starts and goes to the person and Fredshon at 8:00 AM, every day. Once it expires, Karina is copied too and the message says the person can’t be scheduled. It only stops when someone marks it renewed — then the new expiry date is written and the clock restarts.
    12. WhatsApp, email, or both. Set the default channel in the header. Every reminder, weekly publish, monthly publish and ad-hoc message goes out on that channel — email gives you a written record for people who don’t watch WhatsApp during a case.
    13. You are the only administrator. Tasks are added by you — typed in here, or forwarded to the agent by WhatsApp and approved by you before they go on the board.

    Not in this version (phase 2)

    Agreed on the call but deliberately parked so the reminder engine ships first.

    1. Credentialing tracker — BLS, HIPAA, sexual-harassment, license and board expirations per person, with the “10 people’s BLS expires” nag that runs daily until each box is checked.
    2. Turnaround-time tracking — the Scheduling tab now covers shifts by location and role (the Connecteam replacement). Still to come: case time and turnaround estimates per procedure, so the schedule can tell you how many cases a room can actually take.
    3. Quarterly optimization view — turnaround-time trend to present at the quarterly meeting, plus the cost line showing what the Connect Team subscription is replaced by.
    4. Course delivery — click-through HIPAA / harassment / compliance modules that check themselves off when completed.
